04 · The line

What this service will not do.

Worth reading before you pay anyone for statistical help — including us.

We don’t write your thesis

Literature review, discussion, the narrative framed as your own analysis — that’s your work, and your program is right to require it.

We don’t promise outcomes

No guaranteed grade, defense result, or panel approval. Anyone promising those is selling you something else.

We don’t replace your adviser

The analysis is ours to get right. The study is yours, and your adviser’s guidance on it stands.

We don’t invent numbers

No data, no results. If a dataset isn’t ready, you get methodology — never a table of figures that came from nowhere.

05 · Questions

Questions people actually ask.

What file formats can you work with?

Excel, CSV, a Google Forms export, SPSS .sav, or Stata. If your data is still on paper or spread across sheets, say so in the enquiry — cleaning it is part of the scope, and it changes the estimate.

Which software do you use?

Whatever your program expects to see — SPSS, Stata, or JASP, with data prepared in Excel where that’s how it arrives. Output is reported in APA-style tables unless your institution specifies its own format.

Can you just write the results and discussion chapters for me?

No. You get the analysis, the tables, and an interpretation you can understand and defend; putting that into your thesis in your own words is your work. If a request crosses that line, we’ll say so rather than quietly do it.

How does payment work?

An invoice with a fixed amount and a due date, paid by GCash. Send the reference number and work starts from that confirmation. No subscriptions, no hourly billing you can’t predict.

What if my adviser asks for changes?

One round of clarifying questions after delivery is included. A genuinely different analysis — new variables, a changed design — is quoted separately, so you always know what a revision costs before it happens.

Is my data confidential?

Yes. Your dataset and your topic aren’t shared, reused as examples, or shown to other clients. Files are kept only as long as your project needs them.

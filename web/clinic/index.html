Questions

Before you book

Which city will I be seen in?

Whichever one you book into. Dr. Inciong is in Las Piñas one week and Cagayan de Oro the next — the calendar above shows which. Your reminder always carries the address for that week.

What if I need to be seen on a week you are not in my city?

Tell us and we will offer the nearest week that is. We will not book you into a week the clinic is 800 km away, and we will not ask you to fly.

Do I need a referral?

No. Book a new consultation and bring any records or imaging you already have.

Does acupuncture hurt?

Most people feel a dull heaviness rather than a sharp pain. Tell your practitioner during the session and they will adjust.

Can I get a medical certificate?

Yes — for a visit that happened, issued and signed by the practitioner who saw you. We do not issue one without a consultation.

How do I pay?

[PAYMENT CHANNEL]. The amount and the instructions come on your invoice — we never send payment details by unprompted SMS.

What if I need to cancel?

Is this urgent care?
